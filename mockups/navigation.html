Navigation — four takes

Each frame shows the same moment: you're standing at Drudge Hideout in Holtburg, and you want to get back to town. Today that means noticing a sidebar button labelled "→ Holtburg" — while you are, in every meaningful sense, already in Holtburg. The Town panel isn't on screen at all: it only renders when you're already standing in it.

A · Breadcrumb bar

A persistent strip at the top of the Battle tab naming where you are, one clickable segment per level. Town is the region segment — clicking "Holtburg" walks you back to the hub. Smallest change of the four; everything below it stays exactly as it is today.

› › Drudge Hideout
+ Always visible, teaches the region→place model, one obvious "back to town" affordance.   − Another persistent bar in a UI we just spent effort compressing.

B · One "Where to" panel

Regions, Points of Interest and Town collapse into a single destination panel with a segmented control. Town becomes a peer of the hunting grounds rather than a section that appears and disappears. Three sections become one.

+ One panel instead of three; town is never hidden.   − The band strip now mixes two kinds of thing (a place, and a filter).

C · Town is just another card

Leave the structure alone and add Holtburg Town as a pinned card at the head of the POI grid, in every band. Going home becomes the same gesture as going anywhere else — no new concept, no new bar, nothing to learn.

+ Zero new UI; the town gets a quest marker like everything else; travel time to town is finally visible.   − Takes a grid slot in every band.

D · Sidebar becomes the map

Move all travel into the sidebar as a live list of everywhere you can go, grouped by region, with the current spot highlighted. The Battle tab drops its Regions and POI sections entirely and becomes only the fight, the town panel, and the log.
