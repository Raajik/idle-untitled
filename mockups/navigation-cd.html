D's sidebar map, carrying C's information without becoming a wall of text. Three moves: the row is the summary (band colour, level, travel time, marker), the hover card is the detail (everything a location card said, on demand), and bands collapse so a long region is a few rows rather than fifteen.

The map at rest

Every row is four facts wide: level band (the colour of its left edge and the number), name, travel time in the same red/white/green as the cards, and a corner marker when something wants doing there. Town leads in gold. Bands you aren't working collapse to one line.

Hovering a row — C's card, on demand

Everything the location card carried, shown only when you ask for it — and the card is sized to its contents and never wraps, because a tooltip that folds a monster's name onto two lines is harder to read than a wide one. Inhabitants and weaknesses sit side by side: a place can hold three different species, so one shared "weaknesses" line would be a lie. The bold entry is the primary (+45%); the dimmed pair behind it are the +30% and +15%.
